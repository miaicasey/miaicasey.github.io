Flatness vs Projection

ARCHITECTURE / MODULE 01

Exploring the future of living through axonometric drawing, spatial projection and colour.

DISCIPLINE

Architecture & spatial design

TOOLS & METHODS

  • Axonometric drawing
  • Adobe Illustrator
Original study

Context

The theme of my project explores the potentialities within the future of living. How the present can act as a foundation for the multifarious possibilities the future may hold.

Spatial construction

Despite the many forms which the future can take, we still need to respect the laws that govern our world. To honor this I distinctly separated the projections into 4 sections and marked the height between the levels with an axonometric cube to ensure accuracy. Drawing out all the elements in axonometric from back to front to accurately respect the ‘laws‘. I could then command more control over the hidden space by having the elements speak to each other.

The use of color not only honors the elevations but provides depth to the elements. I purposely chose to only use the orange building blocks against the green features to enable the colors to stand out as they are almost complimentary colors.

Design process

These are depictions of some of the elements I struggled with most. Additionally, on the right, there was a change in the creative direction of the coin however as it was not harmonious with the other elements, thus I reverted it back.

Developing the axonometric I experimented with how the ground could be used to speak to the other elements as seen in the bottom left and the placement of objects such as the coin.

Reflection

This was my first time using Illustrator which posed as a challenge however through restarting on 3 seperate occasions I added grid lines, created axonometric boxes in illustrator as well, and pressed shift throughout the development.

Originally eye-dropping the colors from the original elevations I found it was not pleasing to the eye and created a lot of conflict with the elements. Thus to promote more harmony between the elements I experimented with different shades of the original base colors.
