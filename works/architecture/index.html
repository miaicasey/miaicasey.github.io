Context

Three architecture studies explore how a drawing becomes a space, how a surface responds to its surroundings, and how narrative changes the way we experience a place.

The work moves from axonometric projection in Flatness vs Projection, through terrain and fabric studies in Pattern vs Surface, to a spatial reading of Italo Calvino’s Invisible Cities in Frame vs Field.

Approach

Drawing, digital modelling and collage connect each study. Each project’s original reflections trace the experiments, revisions and decisions behind the final work.
