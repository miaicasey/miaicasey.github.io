Context and problem

About 25,000 people had submitted a query to the unauthenticated Evidence chat with very low signup conversion, and about 35,000 a month reached the signup page without an account. Let a visitor do the work before they make an account.

Discovery, explorations and QA

I prototyped browser transcription myself, scoped a backend approach using PromptTeams transcripts, got legal and compliance green light, wireframed the transcribe-then-sign-up flow, and ran two design iterations with the contract designer and the Product Designer. A production API key and 24-hour session deletion landed. SEO tool pages and Google Ads build demand around it.

Impact

  • Legal and compliance approval, production API key and deletion policy in place. No live traffic yet, so no conversion read.

Reflection

Prototyping the browser transcription myself got legal and compliance to a yes faster than a document would have, because they could try it. The project is feature complete and parked, which counts as an outcome: capacity moved to Heidi 2 and this was the right thing to pause.

I would change the order. SEO pages and ads went out ahead of the experience that would convert them, and a smaller version of the flow behind a flag could have run on that traffic during the pause. I would also have asked for the 24-hour deletion rule and the API key before design rather than after, since those were the real gates.

I keep the proof that a product manager can turn a six-week scoping conversation into a working demo in a few days, and that the demo changes the conversation.
