My contribution

I joined Heidi on the Growth product team with a brief to fix onboarding in emerging markets, and over nine months moved from intern to Associate Product Manager to Product Manager. By the end I owned six initiatives: the Patient App, Early User Journeys, Localisation Foundation, the Arabic rollout, Community Routines and the new commercial model for Heidi 2. I shipped a rebuilt onboarding flow, Heidi’s first patient-facing product, Heidi’s first right-to-left language, the most requested enterprise feature of the year, and the growth side of the Heidi 2 launch while the Growth PM and Principal Product Designer were on leave.

I work close to the design. I open most projects by wireframing the flow in Figma so the designer starts from a concrete proposal rather than a paragraph, then iterate with them in the file, and I run design QA against every build before it ships. On the measurement side I write the events with the ticket, build the Amplitude dashboards the team reads from, and report the defensible number even when it is smaller than the first read.

Reflection

Nine months at Heidi taught me that the fastest way to earn a product bet is to measure it in a way nobody can argue with, and to say so when the number does not hold. Twice I pulled results I had already reported and replaced them with the defensible read. I start most projects by drawing the flow before I write the brief, and I sign off the build against the design before it ships. The work I am proudest of sits where product, compliance and data meet: a patient app that stays outside device classification by design, a pricing test on real clinicians with real charges, and an onboarding flow whose dashboards the team still reads from.
